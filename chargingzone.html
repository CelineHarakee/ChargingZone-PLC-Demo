Welcome!

Thank you for connecting! This captive portal introduces our research project that explores the integration of AI into Powerline Communication (PLC) networks, aiming to enhance security, optimize performance, and enable intelligent monitoring.

About the Project

Powerline Communication (PLC) uses electrical wiring for data transmission but faces security risks, interference, and limited optimization. AI techniques enhance PLC by improving security, optimizing bandwidth, and minimizing disruptions.

Research Objectives

  • Analyze common security threats and challenges in PLC networks.
  • Develop AI models to detect intrusions and anomalies in real-time.
  • Design a framework for intelligent monitoring and automatic network tuning.

Importance of the Research

PLC has great potential for smart homes, IoT, and industrial systems, but it faces serious challenges in security and reliability. Our research shows how integrating AI can make PLC a more secure, and efficient communication solution for the future.
